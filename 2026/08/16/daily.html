🥇 Anthropic’s bio-weapons filter was down for nearly a year, exposing 133 million requests

Anthropic’s safety filter for bio-weapons queries failed silently from September 2025 to July 2026 — the company says no evidence of misuse, but the lapse went unnoticed until an internal audit.

Why it matters: Silent failures in safety systems erode trust faster than publicized breaches.

Optima tackles AI benchmarking’s biggest flaw by letting users test models against their own data

Optima launched a platform where users can evaluate AI models using their own datasets — a direct challenge to generic leaderboards that often misrepresent real-world performance.

Why it matters: Custom benchmarks could shift power from model providers to enterprise buyers.

OpenAI dissolved the team built to catch catastrophic AI risks, reassigning its work to other groups

OpenAI quietly disbanded its “Preparedness” team — the group tasked with preventing extreme risks like autonomous weapons or AI-driven pandemics — and folded its work into other teams.

Why it matters: The move signals a pivot from proactive safety to reactive scaling.

One in five US workers now delegates tasks to AI instead of colleagues, survey finds

20% of US workers now assign tasks to AI tools instead of human coworkers — up from 8% in 2025 — with younger employees leading the shift.
