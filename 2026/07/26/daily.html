🥇 Anthropic’s Opus 5 blows past Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol on intelligence benchmark

Anthropic’s Opus 5 outperformed Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol on ARC-AGI, a benchmark designed to measure real-world reasoning — the first time a model has led by a clear margin on this test.

Why it matters: ARC-AGI’s focus on novel problem-solving may signal a shift beyond rote benchmark chasing.

Monday.com is the latest tech company to blame AI for layoffs

Monday.com cited AI as a factor in its latest round of job cuts, joining at least 20 other tech firms this year — a trend accelerating as automation reshapes workflows.

Why it matters: The narrative around AI-driven efficiency is now directly tied to workforce reductions.

The AI coding tutor paradox grows as educators scramble to rethink testing

Educators are overhauling how they assess coding skills as AI tutors make traditional exams obsolete — shifting toward project-based and real-time evaluations.
